# ContextIQ V2 review walkthrough

Open [ContextIQ](http://localhost:8000/) on the preview machine. Select Priya and finish the short setup. The workspace starts with ₹6,000 and a ₹3,500 hostel bill. Preferences lets you switch languages or create a fresh demo workspace. An independent browser profile gets a separate workspace.

1. Open [BrightMart](http://127.0.0.1:8001/). Add one Studio Pro headphones to the store cart. **Track in ContextIQ** sends an item snapshot to **Before Payment**. **Continue to payment** sends a separate checkout snapshot to **During Payment**. From checkout, create an invoice. In the fresh Priya workspace, the review shows the ₹999 shortfall. Select **Consider Everyday headphones** to create a new invoice version. It leaves ₹501 after the purchase and hostel bill.
2. Review the revised invoice. Check the exact authorization box and **Demo response interruption**. Select **Pay with mock provider** and use the demo confirmation code shown on screen. The app verifies the existing capture. In **Activity → Payment Issues**, open the recovery record. **Home** shows ₹4,001, and **Transaction History** has one expense for the purchase.
3. Under **Purchases → After Payment**, open the headphones. Select **Simulate delivery**, enter two days, and confirm. The item has five days left in the replacement window. Enter a fictional defect in **Request replacement** and approve it using the demo seller control. Open the complete briefcase to review the agreement, evidence, and timeline.
4. Open the [merchant desk](http://localhost:8000/#/merchant) to see the corresponding order. Seller delivery and refund controls are labelled as demo actions. **Connect with seller** runs the scoped agent exchange in the same workspace.
5. Use **Create Dynamic QR** on Home for an amount request, or create an itemized QR from the cart. Copy the QR text into **Scan & Pay** to review the signed details. Scanning alone cannot initiate a payment. Try a UPI QR to see the external-code interpretation.
6. Try **Budget** for bills, savings, allocations, installment estimates and the cash-flow forecast. Choose the Kavya persona in a fresh browser profile for personal and business views. Business receivables stay separate from the cash ledger until explicitly recorded as received.
7. Try **Ask** in English, Hindi, or Kannada and follow its record link. Use **Notices → Drafts** to review a message before sending it within the demo.

For the Kirana demo, select **Kavya**. Her business workspace starts with ₹65,000, a ₹12,000 restock bill, and a separate ₹2,400 receivable. **Today's to-do list** highlights one **Next up** task. Use its link to open the exact record. Checking it off saves progress for the day and advances the highlight. Only urgent issues, such as an unresolved payment or a bill due today, can also show a pop-up. Each urgent item is shown once per day in the browser. Expand **What the agent covers** for shortcuts. **Before Payment** monitors supported public product pages and adds a new-low alert when it observes a strictly lower price.

BrightMart transactions in this preview use the mock provider or configured Razorpay test credentials. An external UPI QR may open a compatible UPI app for the user to review. ContextIQ keeps that external transaction unverified. Localhost links are available on the machine running the preview.
